Bagdogra to Hyderabad

Depart Darjeeling 3.5 hours before flight to Bagdogra Airport (IXB). Wear a breathable cotton t-shirt underneath outer layers to comfortably transition into warm 26°C Hyderabad weather upon landing.

Essential Travel & Altitude Notes

Back to top
Permits & Identification (Verify Before Travel) Aadhaar cards are frequently rejected for Protected Area Permits at East Sikkim checkpoints. Carry physical original Voter IDs, Passports, or Driving Licenses alongside 6 passport-size photographs per traveler.
Sub-Zero Battery Care & Cabin Rules Freezing temperatures at Nathula and Tiger Hill drain phone and camera batteries rapidly. Store electronics in inner body-warm pockets, and pack all power banks strictly in your cabin hand luggage for the IXB–HYD flight.
